Three taps, one conversation.

Why YapRoll

Nobody scrolls past you, because nobody scrolls.

There are no profiles to browse and no way to look somebody up. The only way to meet a person on YapRoll is to be paired with them, which means the only people who ever see your profile are the ones you are already talking to.

  • Symmetric filters. The same preferences decide who you find and who can find you.
  • Text and voice. Type, or hold to record a voice note. No video, no photos in chat.
  • Online means online. You are paired with people whose app is open right now.
  • Yours to end. Delete a conversation and it is gone for both of you. Delete your account and everything follows within 30 days.
  • Just a Google account. No phone number, no password to remember.

Safety

Strangers, on purpose. Safe, by design.

18 and over

Your date of birth is confirmed when you sign up and never shown to anyone. An account under 18 cannot be created.

Every photo is checked first

A profile photo is reviewed automatically before anybody else can see it. Borderline ones go to a person. Rejected ones are removed at once.

Report and Block, always

Both are one tap away in every conversation. A blocked person cannot reach you again and is never told they were blocked.

Real moderation

A report goes to a moderator with the conversation's last twenty messages, frozen at that moment. Actions on an account are shown to its owner in the app.

Ad-free

Free with ads. Or not.

The free tier shows a banner and an occasional full-screen ad. The ad-free subscription removes both, and keeps your account out of the inactivity clean-up. Monthly or annual, sold and managed through Google Play, with prices shown in the store before you buy.

Manage or cancel any time

Subscriptions are handled by Google Play. Open Play's subscription page to change or cancel, or use Manage subscription in the app's Settings.
