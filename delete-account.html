Delete your account

YapRoll · Last updated 19 September 2026

You can delete your YapRoll account, and everything it holds, from inside the app. If you no longer have the app, you can ask us by email. Either way the same thing happens: your account is hidden at once and permanently deleted 30 days later.

In the app

  1. Open YapRoll and go to Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. You are signed out.

From that moment your profile is hidden, you cannot be paired with anyone, and every conversation you were in disappears from the other person's chats as well as yours.

You have 30 days to change your mind. Sign in again with the same Google account within 30 days and you will be offered the option to restore everything exactly as it was. After 30 days the deletion is permanent and nothing can be recovered.

Without the app

If you have uninstalled YapRoll, lost the device, or cannot sign in, either:

  • Reinstall the app, sign in with the Google account you used, and delete it from Settings as above; or
  • Email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the Google account's email address, with the subject Delete my account. We start the same 30-day deletion and confirm by reply. We ask that the email comes from the address on the account so nobody else can delete it for you.

What is deleted

When the 30 days are up, the following are permanently removed:

  • your profile — name, date of birth, sex, region, languages and every photo, including the photo review scores;
  • your search preferences;
  • every conversation you were in, with all of its messages and voice notes, for both people;
  • your presence record, push tokens and app language;
  • your block list, the reports you filed and the reports filed about you, with their evidence copies, and any moderation actions and notices on the account;
  • the record of your ad-free subscription, if you had one;
  • your Google account link. Your Google account itself is not affected.

Voice notes and photos are removed from storage within an hour of the account being purged. Backups that still contain the data are overwritten in the ordinary course of operation and are never used to restore a deleted account.

What may be kept

  • Anything we are legally required to preserve — for example, a child-safety report made to the authorities — is kept for as long as the law requires, and for nothing else.
  • Records that Google Play keeps about a subscription purchase are Google's; deleting your YapRoll account does not delete your Play purchase history.

Deleting some things without deleting the account

  • A conversation — open it, tap the name at the top, and choose Delete chat. It is removed for both of you.
  • A message — long-press it and choose delete for yourself or for both.
  • A photo — remove it in the profile editor. The file is gone within the hour.
  • Your preferences, name, region or languages — change them in the profile editor at any time.

Your subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel an ad-free subscription. Subscriptions are billed by Google Play, not by us. Cancel it on Google Play's subscriptions page before or after deleting, or it will keep renewing.

Questions

Anything about deletion, or a request for a copy of your data before it goes, to [CONTACT EMAIL]. The Privacy Policy lists everything we hold and why.
