Privacy Policy

YapRoll · Effective 1 October 2026

What changed in this version. This version names the company behind YapRoll and gives everybody the privacy rights of the GDPR and of California law, wherever they live. It also covers a lot the old policy left out, from your sign-in record, reactions, edits, replies and GIFs to the typing indicator, session and server logs, the iPhone app, the website, sensitive information and the fingerprint kept of a banned account. And deleting your account now deletes your sign-in record too.

YapRoll is a chat app for adults. You tap Search, you are paired with somebody else who is online and matches your preferences, and that pairing becomes a conversation. This policy explains what YapRoll collects to make that work, why, who else handles it, how long it is kept, and what you can do about it.

In short, we collect what we need to pair you with people and to keep your conversations working and safe, and we do not sell your data. Your messages are seen by the person you are talking to, and by a moderator only if somebody reports the conversation. You can delete everything at any time.

1. Who we are

YapRoll is run by Bros Dolz LLC, 418 Broadway STE N, Albany, NY 12207, United States (“we”, “us”). We are the controller of your personal data: we decide what is collected and why. Contact us about anything in this policy at support@yaproll.app.

One standard for everybody. We give every user the rights in the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and in the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), wherever they live. If the law where you live gives you more, you get that too.

Sensitive information

What you tell us about your sex, and about who you want to meet and why, can reveal something about your sexual orientation or your sex life. The GDPR and some other laws give that kind of information extra protection, so we ask for your explicit consent before we use it. When you create your account, the app explains this and asks you to tap “I agree”, and we record when you did. If you joined before we added this step, the app will ask you once.

We use it to pair you, and for nothing else. Only the people you are paired with see it. It is never used for advertising, and advertisers never get it. You can change your answers whenever you like in your profile and preferences. But pairing cannot work without them, so withdrawing your consent means deleting your account, after which everything is deleted as described in section 9.

4. Automated photo review

Every profile photo you add is checked automatically before anybody else can see it. The photo is sent to OpenAI's moderation service, which returns scores for sexual, violent and self-harm content. Photos that score clearly low are shown and photos that score clearly high are removed. Anything in between waits for a moderator.

A removed photo counts against your account, and enough of them lead automatically to a warning and then to suspensions, as set out in the Community Guidelines. Because these decisions can be made without a person, you always have the right to have a person review them: appeal as described in section 5 of the Terms of Service.

No other decision about you is made automatically. Pairing chooses among the people who match both sets of preferences, without any profiling.

5. Ads

The free tier shows ads from Google AdMob. Before any ad is personalised, you are asked for your consent where the law requires it, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings › Ad preferences, wherever you live. On iPhone, the advertising identifier is used only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. If you do not consent, you still see ads, but they are not based on your activity.

We never give advertisers your profile, your messages, your preferences or anything you tell us. What AdMob collects, it collects directly from your device under Google's own terms. The ad-free subscription removes ads, and with them AdMob, entirely.

7. Who can see what

  • Your profile, meaning your display name, age, sex, what you are looking for, region, languages, approved photos and whether you are online, is visible only to the people you are paired with. Strangers cannot look you up, and there is no public profile.
  • Your messages are visible to the other person in the conversation. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted: they are stored on our servers so they can be delivered to the other person's devices, and so that a reported conversation can be reviewed.
  • Moderators can see reports, the evidence attached to them, photos waiting for review, and your account's moderation history. They cannot read your conversations otherwise, and every time they open report evidence, it is recorded.
  • A person you block is not told that you blocked them.
  • If you delete your account, your photos and online status are hidden straight away. Until it is permanently deleted, the people you were talking to still see your display name and your conversation with them, read-only, with a note that you deleted your account.

Your data is stored in the United States, in Supabase's data centre in Oregon. The services in section 6 may also process it in the United States and other countries. Where data leaves the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, those services protect it with the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

10. How we protect it

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit, and our database and file storage are encrypted at rest. Access rules in the database mean each account can read only what it is allowed to see. Only moderators can reach safety records, through tools that record what they open. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach ever puts your data at risk, we will tell you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

11. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can:

  • see the personal data we hold about you, and get a copy of it in a common, machine-readable format;
  • correct it: most of it you can edit yourself in your profile and preferences;
  • delete it: under Settings › Delete account, or by emailing us;
  • restrict or object to how we use it, including anything we do on the basis of legitimate interest;
  • withdraw consent at any time, for personalised ads under Settings › Ad preferences, and for sensitive information by deleting your account, without affecting what was done before;
  • ask for a person to review any automated decision (section 4);
  • exercise any of these rights without being treated any differently for doing so; and
  • complain to the data protection authority where you live. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

How to ask. Email support@yaproll.app from the email address of your YapRoll account, so we know the request is yours. You can also ask somebody to make a request on your behalf, if they can show they are authorised to. We answer within one month. If a request is complicated, we may take up to two more months, and we will tell you why within the first month. It is free.

12. Age

YapRoll is for adults only. An account cannot be created with a date of birth under 18, and an account we find to belong to somebody under 18 is deleted. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child is using YapRoll, report the account in the app with the reason Underage, or email support@yaproll.app.

13. The website

yaproll.app uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising. If you use the theme switch, your choice is remembered in your own browser and is never sent to us. The site is hosted by GitHub, which sees your IP address when you visit, as any web host does.

14. Changes to this policy

We may change this policy. When we do, we update the date at the top and tell you in the app the next time you open it, with a notice you confirm you have seen. The current version is always the one on this page.
