Privacy Policy

YapRoll · Last updated 15 September 2026

YapRoll is a chat app for adults. You tap Search, you are paired with somebody else who is online and matches your preferences, and that pairing becomes a conversation. This page says what YapRoll collects to do that, why, who else touches it, how long it is kept, and how to have it deleted.

2. Who else touches it

YapRoll runs on services we do not operate ourselves. Each one processes your data only to do the job named here, under its own agreements with us.

  • Supabase — hosts the database, the files (photos and voice notes) and the server code. Everything in section 1 lives here.
  • Google — Google Sign-In (your account), Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications), Google Play Billing (subscriptions) and Google AdMob (ads on the free tier).
  • OpenAI — every profile photo you add is sent to OpenAI's moderation service, which returns scores for sexual, violent and self-harm content. The photo is sent for that classification only, under OpenAI's API data-use terms, and is not used to train their models.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone else except as the law requires — see section 5.

3. Who can see what

  • Your profile — name, sex, languages, region, approved photos, and whether you are online — is visible to the people you are paired with and to nobody else. Strangers cannot look you up.
  • Your messages are visible to the other person in the conversation.
  • Moderators can see reports, the frozen evidence attached to them, photos held for review, and your account's moderation history. They cannot read your conversations otherwise.
  • A person you block is not told they have been blocked.

4. How long we keep it

  • Deleting your account hides your profile and conversations immediately. You have 30 days to sign back in and undo it; after that everything is permanently deleted.
  • Inactivity — an account on the free tier that has not opened the app for 90 days is permanently deleted.
  • Conversations in which neither person has sent a message for 30 days are deleted for both.
  • Photos and voice notes you remove, and those belonging to deleted conversations or accounts, are removed from storage within an hour.
  • A rejected photo is deleted at once. The scores it received are kept, without the image.
  • Safety records — moderation actions, notices and report evidence — are kept while the accounts involved exist, so a pattern across several reports can be seen.

5. Legal basis and your rights

We process your data to provide the service you signed up for (contract), to keep it safe and lawful (legitimate interest and legal obligation), and — for personalised ads in the EEA, UK and Switzerland — with your consent.

You can, at any time:

  • Delete your account from Settings → Delete account, or by emailing [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address on the account.
  • Change or withdraw ad consent under Settings → Ad preferences.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or object to how it is used, by emailing [CONTACT EMAIL].
  • Complain to your data-protection authority.

We may disclose data where the law requires it, including reporting child sexual abuse material to the relevant authorities.

6. Age

YapRoll is for people 18 and over. An account cannot be created with a date of birth under 18, and an account found to belong to somebody under 18 is removed.

7. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will tell you before the change takes effect. The date at the top is the date of the current version.
