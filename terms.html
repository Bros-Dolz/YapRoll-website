2. What YapRoll is

YapRoll pairs you with another person who is online and matches your search preferences, and turns that pairing into a conversation of text, voice notes, GIFs and reactions. Your preferences work both ways: the age range, sexes, languages and regions you choose also decide who can be paired with you.

We do not check anybody's identity. The people you meet are strangers, and you should treat them that way. What they tell you about themselves may not be true. If you decide to meet somebody in person, that is your decision and your responsibility; the safety tips in the Community Guidelines are worth reading first.

3. The main rules

The full rules, with examples, are in the Community Guidelines. These are the ones that matter most. They apply to everything you send, post or show on YapRoll: messages, voice notes, GIFs, reactions, your display name, your profile and your photos.

  • Zero tolerance for anything that sexualises or endangers a child. We remove it, ban every account involved without warning or appeal, and report it to the relevant authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children. See our Child Safety Standards.
  • No inappropriate content: no nudity or sexual content, no violence or gore, no hate speech, and nothing that promotes self-harm, suicide or eating disorders.
  • No harassment. No threats, intimidation, bullying, or carrying on after somebody has asked you to stop.
  • No spam, scams or selling. Do not ask people for money, sell anything (including sexual services), or advertise.
  • Be yourself. Do not impersonate anybody or lie about your age.
  • Keep conversations private. Do not share a conversation, or anything from it, outside YapRoll without the other person's consent. See section 7.
  • Nothing illegal, where you are or where the other person is.

4. Moderation

Report and block. Every conversation has a Report button and a Block button. A person you block cannot reach you again and is not told that you blocked them. When you report a conversation, a copy of its last 20 text messages and GIFs is attached to the report, frozen at that moment, so a moderator can see what happened even if the messages are deleted afterwards.

We review every report within 24 hours, and a person makes the decision every time.

Photos are checked before anybody sees them. Every profile photo goes through an automated check first. Photos that are clearly fine go up, and photos that clearly break the rules are removed. If the check is not sure either way, the photo waits for a moderator. Photos that keep getting removed lead to the steps in the Community Guidelines, ending with a review of the account by a moderator.

What we can do. Depending on how serious a breach is, we may remove content, warn you, suspend your account for a period, or ban it. A suspended or banned account cannot search for new people or send messages, and other people cannot send messages to it; it can still read its existing conversations. We may act on reports, on the automated photo check, or on our own findings.

You will always be told why. Every action we take on your account appears in the app under Settings › Account notices, with the rule it was taken under and, for a suspension, when it ends.

5. Appeals

If you think we got a decision wrong, you can appeal it.

  • How: email support@yaproll.app from the email address of your YapRoll account, say which decision you are appealing, and tell us why you think it was wrong.
  • When: within 30 days of the decision appearing in your Account notices. We cannot consider an appeal sent after that.
  • Answer: within 14 days of receiving your appeal. A moderator who looks at it afresh will either confirm the decision or reverse it. If we reverse it, the restriction is lifted and you get a notice in the app saying so.
  • This also covers decisions made by the automated photo check: you can always ask for a person to look again.
  • No appeal for child sexual abuse. Bans for sexual content involving a minor, or for sexualising, grooming or exploiting a minor, are final.

10. Deleting your account

  • You can delete your account at any time under Settings › Delete account. You are signed out, nobody can be paired with you or send you messages, and your photos and online status are hidden straight away. The people you were talking to keep the conversation, read-only, with a note that you deleted your account.
  • You then have 30 days to change your mind: sign back in and choose Reactivate. After 30 days, your account and everything in it is permanently deleted, including your sign-in record.
  • Inactive accounts: an account on the free tier that has not opened the app for 90 days is permanently deleted.
  • Quiet conversations: a conversation in which neither person has sent a message for 30 days is deleted for both.
  • Some safety records are kept after deletion, as the Privacy Policy explains. In particular, if your account was banned, we keep a one-way fingerprint of your sign-in account so that a ban cannot be undone by deleting the account and signing up again.
