4. How the rules are enforced

  • Report and block. Every conversation has a Report button and a Block button. A blocked person cannot reach you again and is not told they were blocked. A report goes to a moderator with a copy of the conversation's last 20 messages, taken at the moment you report.
  • Photos. A rejected photo is removed. Repeated rejections lead, in order, to a warning, a one-day suspension, a seven-day suspension, a thirty-day suspension, and then review by a moderator.
  • Actions. A moderator may remove content, warn you, suspend your account for a period, or ban it. Every action except a dismissed report is shown to you in the app, under Settings → Account notices. A suspended or banned account cannot search or send messages, and other people cannot send messages to it.
  • We may act on our own findings as well as on reports. We are not obliged to give a reason beyond the notice shown in the app.
